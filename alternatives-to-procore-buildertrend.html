Affordable alternatives to Procore & Buildertrend

Strong tools, built for a different job than most small contractors actually have.

Procore and Buildertrend are built for a contractor running a large crew or multiple commercial jobs at once. For a small or solo residential contractor who mainly needs accurate pricing, job costing and cash flow visibility, the subscription and the learning curve on either one can be hard to justify.

What you give up

This isn't a fair fight on features, and it shouldn't be read as one. Procore and Buildertrend both do live multi-user project tracking, client portals and scheduling that a spreadsheet simply doesn't. If that's what you actually need, the subscription is the right tool. KliWorks is built for the narrower job of knowing your real margin before you quote, tracking job costs against budget while the job runs, and seeing your cash flow gap before you sign the contract — without the monthly cost.
