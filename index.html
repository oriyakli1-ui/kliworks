What cost engineering means here

A spreadsheet that multiplies a cost by 1.2 is not a cost model. A cost model states where every dollar of overhead is recovered, loads labor for the burden it genuinely carries, and derives a price from a margin you chose rather than a number that felt about right.

That distinction is the whole product. The arithmetic in these guides is the same arithmetic inside the files, which is why the guides give it away in full.
